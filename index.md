# Applying AI to real operating problems

I’m a strategy and operations leader building hands-on capability in applied and agentic AI.

My focus is not technology for its own sake. I’m interested in how AI, agents, and automation can be applied to messy business processes, improve how work gets done, and create measurable value.

This site documents what I’m building, what I’m learning, and how I’m thinking about AI-enabled value creation.

## Building now

### Family AI Chief of Staff

With three kids and two parents, school emails, sports schedules, deadlines, activities, and parent handoffs create a surprisingly messy coordination problem.

I’m experimenting with whether an AI-enabled workflow can turn that fragmented information into a more reliable household operating system.

The first version starts simple:

- ingest synthetic school and activity emails
- extract events, deadlines, and required actions
- structure the information consistently
- identify what each parent needs to know or do

From there, I plan to add persistence, calendar awareness, ownership, proposed actions, recurring briefs, human approvals, and increasingly agentic behavior.

## What I’m exploring

- Identifying AI opportunities with meaningful operational or financial value
- Translating messy business processes into AI-enabled workflows
- Choosing the right models, tools, agents, and automation stack for a problem
- Designing human-in-the-loop workflows, controls, and governance
- Driving adoption and changing how teams actually work
- Building practical playbooks for scaling AI across an organization

## Background

My background spans strategy consulting and hands-on operating roles, with a focus on growth, value creation, transformation, and cross-functional execution.

The common thread has been taking ambiguous problems, structuring them, and moving from analysis to implementation. I’m now applying that same approach to AI.
