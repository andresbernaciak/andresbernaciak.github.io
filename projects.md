## Family AI Chief of Staff

**Status:** In progress

With three kids and two parents, school emails, sports schedules, deadlines, activities, and parent handoffs create a surprisingly messy coordination problem.

I’m building an AI-enabled workflow to turn that fragmented information into structured events, actions, deadlines, and eventually a more reliable household operating system.

The first version focuses on:

- extracting events, deadlines, and required actions from synthetic school and activity emails
- structuring the information consistently
- identifying what parents need to know or do
- creating a foundation for calendar awareness, ownership, approvals, and recurring briefs
