# About

I’m a strategy and operations leader with 15+ years of experience helping organizations solve complex growth, performance, and operating challenges.

My career has included both strategy consulting and hands-on operating roles, working with executive teams across large corporations, middle-market businesses, and private equity-backed companies.

Much of my work has followed the same pattern:

- take an ambiguous or poorly defined problem
- structure it into something that can be analyzed and acted on
- identify where value can be created
- align people across functions
- move from recommendation to execution

I’ve worked across growth strategy, value creation, transformation, M&A, operating models, and strategic initiatives, often in situations where there was no obvious playbook.

## Why AI

I see AI as a new set of capabilities for solving many of the same operating problems I’ve worked on throughout my career.

The interesting question for me is not simply what a model can generate. It is how AI can be incorporated into real workflows:

- Where can it materially improve economics or performance?
- Which parts of a process should be automated?
- Where does human judgment remain important?
- How should agents interact with systems and data?
- What controls are needed?
- How do you get people to actually adopt a new way of working?
- How does an organization scale successful experiments?

I’m building hands-on capability so I can understand those questions from both a strategic and practical perspective.

## This site

This site is my working portfolio.

I use it to document projects, experiments, architecture decisions, failures, and lessons as I build AI-enabled workflows.

The goal is not to present myself as a software engineer. It is to become a better strategic operator who understands enough of the technology to identify opportunities, prototype solutions, work effectively with technical teams, and help turn AI into measurable business value.

## Earlier technical exploration

My interest in technology predates the current AI cycle.

In 2020, I spent time teaching myself Python, data science, version control, and software-development fundamentals. Those early posts remain on this site as an archive.

Today, that interest is focused much more directly on applied AI, agents, automation, and how those capabilities change the way organizations operate.
